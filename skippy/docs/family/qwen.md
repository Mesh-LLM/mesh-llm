# Qwen family validation

Use the maintained [family certification workflow](../FAMILY_CERTIFY.md) for
model-family qualification and [split serving guide](../SKIPPY_SPLITS.md) for
current deployment instructions.

Exactness checks belong to [skippy-correctness](../../crates/skippy-correctness/README.md).
Interactive diagnostics can collect stage telemetry with
[metrics-server](../../crates/metrics-server/README.md).

The former fixed-lab package-validation runbook and historical experiment
reports have been retired. Their measurements do not establish qualification
for current models, runtimes, or deployment topologies.
