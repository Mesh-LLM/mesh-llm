several companies and the wider community. Affiliations below describe
contribution hats, not ownership of the project. **Community** means no company
contribution affiliation is recorded here; it is not a statement about employment.

## Contributors

Maintainers are only part of the project. Thank you to
[everyone who has contributed](https://github.com/Mesh-LLM/mesh-llm/graphs/contributors),
including corporate and independent/community contributors. Contribution counts
alone do not determine maintainer status.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) to get involved. To correct this list or an
affiliation, open a pull request.
